Graphic Design

Slap Dragon Vinyl Design

Graphic Design

Date: Spring 2026

Class: Digital Imaging Techniques

Overview: I designed and assembled a vinyl cover as well as several merchandise items for a band performing at Festival International 2026. I chose Slap Dragon, a “Groove Grass” band out of Nashville that radiates really upbeat, whimsical energy in their music. I wanted the visual elements and the color palette I chose to represent the positive feeling I attribute to their music as well as the whimsy I find in their lyrics.

Project Title

Branding

Date: Spring 2026

Class: Digital Imaging Techniques

Overview: I created a poster for a national park of my choice inspired by WPA style design which features flat, smooth shapes, limited color palettes, and art deco typefaces.

Project Title

Editorial Design

Date: Spring 2026

Class: Digital Imaging Techniques

Overview: This project challenged me to create a series of images based on an everyday object of my choice using a variety of imaging techniques, including both digital and physical methods. For my object, I chose a book. I have always loved reading, and my goal was to portray the feelings and experiences associated with books.
