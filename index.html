Hawai'i Travel Magazine & Poster

Date: Spring 2026

Class: Graphic Design 1

Overview: I created a travel poster for a US state of my choosing that incorporated visual collage, then created a magazine ad that promoted the poster as well as the sponsor. For the poster, I used visual hierarchy to create a multi-layered scene. I chose to fully hand-illustrate all of the elements. The sponsor I chose was @lokahiscuba, a company that offers diving tours in Oahu. Their logo is featured in both the poster as well as the ad. For the ad, because I chose to focus on diving with sea life as a recreational activity, I wanted to highlight the sea life a visitor could expect to see on a dive with Lokahi Scuba. The ad explains that if you book a tour with the company, you will receive a free copy of the poster.

Editorial & Publication

Brochure

Date: Fall 2025

Class: Graphic Abstraction

Overview: I designed a brochure by combining assets from previous projects throughout my Graphic Abstraction class. My goal was to create a unified composition through the use of a monochromatic color palette as well as a continuous line that brings the elements together.

On Par: Minigolf for Beginners

Date: Fall 2025

Class: Graphic Abstraction

Overview: I created a balanced composition using only black and white squares within a grid. The first challenge was to create a traditional grid, which I then broke into a smaller grid, and the second was a diagonal one. Ultimately, I was to use assets from any of my grids in a book cover design. I chose to use pieces from my diagonal grid to create mini golf courses in my imaginary book “On Par: Mini Golf for Beginners.”
